# Sample size and sensitivity for the three-application onboarding study (onb-study/2; DRAFT; NOT RUN)

**Status.** A planning analysis for revision candidate r2's #30 (option A). It uses no participant data, and nobody has
been recruited. The numbers are exact binomial calculations, generated by
`research_tools/onboarding/study3.py power` into `SAMPLE_SIZE_r2.json`, from
`participant_study_3app.sample_size_table` (tested in `tests/experimental/test_participant_study_3app_offline.py`).

## The test

- **Primary outcome:** per participant, M − D in application-standardized end-to-end seconds to S3.
- **Test:** an exact two-sided sign test at α = 0.05 on the pairs whose direction is determined.
- **Effect size:** p, the share of participants for whom one condition is faster. p = 0.5 is no effect.
- **Why a sign test:** each participant's two sessions are on different applications, so the magnitude of a
  difference depends on the standardization. The sign is more robust to that, and to censoring at the 5,400 s
  budget. The cost is power.

## Sensitivity to lost pairs

Pairs are lost when both sessions are censored, when a censored pair's direction is undetermined, or when a session
deviates from its allocated sequence. Each loss lowers the determined n.

| Planned | 10% lost | 20% lost | 30% lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | n = 16, p ≥ 0.853 | n = 14, p ≥ 0.889 | n = 12, p ≥ 0.870 |
| 24 | n = 21, p ≥ 0.809 | n = 19, p ≥ 0.834 | n = 16, p ≥ 0.853 |

(The detectable p is not monotone in n, because the sign test's critical values are discrete.)

## What this justifies, and what it doesn't

- **18 participants** is the smallest number that fills the six sequences evenly (3 each). It detects only a
  large, consistent effect: about 82% or more of participants favouring one condition, for 80% power.
- **24 participants** is preferred. It adds the six mirror sequences, which balance application order, and lowers
  the detectable share to about 80%. With 20% of pairs lost, it still matches 18's no-loss power.
- **Going further:** 30–36 participants would detect p ≈ 0.75. The owner would choose that; the protocol doesn't
  require it.
- **A null result** at any of these sizes is not evidence of no effect. The verdict is INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE below the chosen
  design's minimum (18 for `min18`, 24 for `pref24`), or with fewer than its per-sequence minimum (3 or 2) in any
  sequence. The design is fixed in the frozen manifest before recruitment.
- **The RA-Q4 claim rule** (a median reduction of at least 30% in minutes and lines on all three applications) is a
  separate, descriptive criterion. It is not powered here.

## Not planned here

The effect size is not estimated from the AI-operator pilots (onb-pilot/2 and /3): they had 2 applications,
AI operators rather than independent participants, and (in pilot 2) no checkpoint, so they say nothing reliable
